// frequently asked questions

Corporate AI training, answered.

What is corporate AI training?
Corporate AI training is structured training designed to help employees understand and practically use Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI in their professional roles.
What departments can receive AI training?
AI training can be designed for HR, marketing, sales, finance, operations, customer service, business development and other departments.
Do you offer AI training for HR teams?
Yes. AI for HR can cover areas such as recruitment, onboarding, employee communication, HR documentation, training, performance support and other HR workflows.
Do you offer AI training for marketing and sales teams?
Yes. Training can cover AI-powered research, content creation, campaign planning, customer insights, sales prospecting, outreach, proposals, CRM workflows and reporting.
Do you offer AI training for finance teams?
Yes. AI for Finance can cover applications such as spreadsheet analysis, reporting, financial research, data interpretation, variance analysis and management summaries.
Is corporate AI training suitable for non-technical employees?
Yes. Many business AI applications do not require programming knowledge. Training can be designed around practical no-code and low-code AI workflows.
Can corporate AI training be customized?
Yes. Training can be customized according to the organization's departments, roles, skill levels, objectives, workflows and preferred tools.
Do you provide onsite AI training?
Yes. Corporate training can be arranged onsite depending on the organization's requirements.
Do you offer online corporate AI training?
Yes. Online training can be delivered for teams working from different offices, cities or locations.
Can we train multiple departments?
Yes. Organizations can combine different department-specific tracks into a broader corporate AI training program.
How long does corporate AI training take?
The duration depends on the training objectives, number of participants, department and depth of the curriculum. Programs can be structured as focused workshops, full-day sessions, multi-day programs or longer learning journeys.
Does AI training include practical exercises?
Yes. Depending on the selected program, participants can work through practical exercises, business scenarios, AI tools and role-specific workflows.
