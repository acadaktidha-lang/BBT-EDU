## Identity

- The institute is called **Big Binary Trainings**, usually shortened to **BBT**.
- Big Binary Tech is the parent software house. Big Binary Trainings is the
  training institute, and this website is the institute's.
- Website: https://bbt.edu.pk

## Address, location, how to visit

- Campus address: **444-Q, DHA Phase II, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan**
- Visitors are welcome during working hours; a free counselling session can be
  booked in advance.
- Classes run **onsite in Lahore and online across Pakistan**. Online students
  anywhere in Pakistan can book a video counselling call.

## Opening hours and timings

- Open **Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM**. Closed Sunday.

## Fees, price, cost — IMPORTANT

- **BBT does not publish course fees on its website.** There is no fee list,
  no price page and no figure anywhere that represents a course fee.
- Anyone asking about fees, price, cost, instalments or discounts must be sent
  to admissions on WhatsApp 0326-0188811 or admissions@bbt.edu.pk.
- Course and track pages show **PKR salary ranges**. Those are *expected
  starting salaries for graduates of that field* — they are **not** course fees
  and must never be quoted as a price.
- NAVTTC courses are a separate case: those are government-funded and **free**
  for eligible applicants.

## Accreditation

- NAVTTC-approved; recognised by PSDA and PSEB.
- University and industry links include FAST-NUCES, UMT, Cisco and SOFTEC.
