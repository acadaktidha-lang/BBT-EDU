BBT Kids · Age 12+

Kids Entrepreneurship.

Spot a problem. Build the solution. Pitch it.

Entrepreneurship is identifying problems, creating solutions and bringing ideas to life. This course teaches the process - and uses AI to speed up every step of it.

2 Months·16 Classes·Age 12+·● Certificate

// what you will learn

How a real idea becomes a real pitch.

This one is deliberately more theoretical than technical - the focus is on understanding the process of entrepreneurship rather than coding, so every student can take part.

// what they learn
  • ▪Problem Identification - How to spot real-world problems worth solving, brainstorm openly, then narrow down to something actually solvable.
  • ▪Target Audience & Personas - What a persona is, how to build a simple one, and why teenagers are a target customer worth understanding.
  • ▪Business Models - Value proposition explained, plus subscription, marketplace and freemium models compared with real examples.
  • ▪The Business Model Canvas - A simplified canvas students map their own idea onto, then pull apart in group discussion.
// going further
  • ▪Market Research with AI - How entrepreneurs research markets, which AI tools support it, and a live activity searching trends and data.
  • ▪Pitching & Storytelling - What a pitch is, what makes one strong, turning a problem into a story, and building emotional connection.
  • ▪Pitch Decks with NotebookLM - Key slides - problem, solution, audience, business model - drafted with NotebookLM and reviewed as a group.
  • ▪AI in Presentation Skills - Using AI to improve communication, practise delivery and build confidence before Pitch Day.

// is this right for your child?

Who this is for.

Children aged 12 and up

The course is theory-led rather than code-led, so no technical background is required.

Kids with business ideas already

Week 3 turns a loose idea into a business model canvas that other people can critique.

Students who want to present better

Half the course is pitching, storytelling and delivery, with AI as a practice partner.

Creative problem solvers

Problem identification and brainstorming are the opening modules, before any business talk.

Future founders

Personas, value propositions, market research and pitch decks - the actual working vocabulary.

Kids who freeze in front of an audience

Pitch Day is the destination, and weeks 5 to 8 are the preparation for it.

// kids entrepreneurship faqs

Your questions, answered straight.

Is this a coding course?
No. It is deliberately more theoretical, focused on understanding the process of entrepreneurship rather than building software - which keeps it accessible to every student.
What age is it for?
Children aged 12 and above.
What AI tools are used?
NotebookLM for generating draft pitch decks, plus AI tools for market research, communication practice and feedback.
How long is the course?
Two months: 16 classes, 2 classes per week, 1 hour each.
What is Pitch Day?
The final class. Each student presents their finished pitch to the group, reflects on the journey, and receives their certificate.
Does my child need a business idea to start?
No. Finding one is the job of weeks 2 and 3 - brainstorming problems, then narrowing down to something worth solving.
